Barbados Sailing Week & Mount Gay Round Barbados
Jan 15 — 22
Barbados — Carlisle Bay
🌊 Offshore 🧒 Juniors (Dinghies)
🏝 The Island During the Regatta
Barbados comes alive in peak season. Bridgetown and its historic UNESCO-listed marine area buzz with activity. Mount Gay Rum — the world's oldest rum (since 1703) — is ever-present: the "Mount Gay Rum parties" are legendary after each race day. The west coast beaches (Holetown, Speightstown) and south coast (Dover Beach, Oistins Fish Fry on Friday nights) vibrate with crews from around the world.
⛵ The Race
The highlight is the Mount Gay Round Barbados Race (approx. Jan 21): a full circumnavigation of the island offshore. This is followed by the "Rum to Spice Race" (Jan 22), connecting Barbados to Grenada — a bridge to the Grenada Sailing Week. Dinghy classes are available for under-16s, as well as kiteboards and wing foils.
🌙 Nightlife & Culture
Oistins Fish Fry on Fridays is a must — fresh fish, calypso music, rum, and sunset views. Bridgetown's Harbour Lights and Boat Yard are hotspots for festive nights. Experience the warm and colorful Bajan atmosphere.
Island Tour + Offshore Race · Sponsored by Mount Gay Rum
Grenada Sailing Week
Jan 31 — Feb 5
Grenada & Carriacou
🌊 Offshore
🏝 The Island During the Regatta
The regatta kicks off in Carriacou — Grenada's sister island to the north, accessible by ferry from St. George's. The atmosphere is totally relaxed: beach barbecues every evening, live local music. Grenada is nicknamed the "Isle of Spice" — you'll smell nutmeg and cinnamon as soon as you reach the docks. Over 12 nationalities are represented.
⛵ The Race
Starting in Carriacou, the race goes around the island, followed by an offshore race to Grenada, then coastal races along the south coast. The courses weave between lush green hills and pristine beaches with steady trade winds.
🌙 Evenings & Culture
Gouyave's Fish Friday: a fishing village with fresh seafood and music every Friday evening. Beach BBQs, Caribbean concerts every night. The Aquarium Restaurant is directly on Magazine Beach.
